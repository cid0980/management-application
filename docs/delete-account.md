# Delete your Shop Manager account

You can delete your account at any time.

## From the app (quickest)

1. Open Shop Manager and sign in.
2. Tap **More** (bottom right).
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete my account**.
4. Type your password and tap **Delete for ever**.

If you are the only admin of a shop that still has other staff, make
somebody else an admin first (More → Staff), so the shop is not left without
anyone who can manage it.

## Without the app

Email **[email address]** from the address you sign in with, with the subject
"Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

## What is deleted, and what is kept

- **Deleted:** your login, your email address and your membership of the shop.
- **Kept by the shop:** the business records the shop made while you worked
  there — bills, repairs, stock and customers. They belong to the shop.
  A shop owner who wants the whole shop's records deleted can ask at the
  email above.
