# Privacy policy — Shop Manager

_Last updated: 22 November 2026. Replace the bracketed parts before
publishing._

Shop Manager ("the app") is a management app for mobile phone shops,
published by **[your name or company]** ("we"). This policy says what the app
stores, why, and how to have it deleted.

## Who the app is for

The app is used by shop owners and their staff. Each shop's records are kept
separate; people in one shop cannot see another shop's records.

## What is stored

- **Your account:** your email address, and the name and role (admin or
  staff) your shop gives you.
- **Records your shop types in:** customers' names and phone numbers,
  handsets bought, sold or repaired (including IMEI numbers), bills and
  payments, enquiries, tasks, stock and the shop's settings.
- **Photos you choose to take:** handset photos and, on a purchase, photos of
  the seller's ID card.
- **Ratings:** when a shop asks a customer to rate a repair, the rating and
  any comment the customer writes.

- **Crash reports:** if the app crashes, a report of where in the app it
  happened, the app's build, the phone model and Android version, and your
  account id (not your email or anything your shop typed), so the fault can
  be fixed.

The app does not read your location, messages or call log. It reads your
phone's contacts only when you choose "Import contacts", and saves only the
ones you tick; it can also read a contacts file you pick.

## Why

Only to run the shop: to show and print records, bills, job cards and labels,
and to let staff of the same shop work on the same records.

## Where it is kept

Records are stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase
Authentication); crash reports go to Firebase Crashlytics. Photos are stored with Cloudinary. Both act as service
providers for the app and receive data over encrypted connections. We do not
sell data or share it for advertising.

## Customers of a shop

If you are a customer of a shop that uses the app, the shop decides what it
records about you. Ask the shop to correct or remove your details.

## Deleting your account

- In the app: **More → Delete my account**.
- Or on the web: **[link to the delete-account page]**.

Deleting your account removes your login and your membership of the shop.
The shop's business records (bills, stock, customers) belong to the shop and
are kept by it, as businesses are generally required to keep sales records.
A shop owner who wants all of the shop's records deleted can write to us at
**[email]**.

## Children

The app is for businesses and is not directed at children.
